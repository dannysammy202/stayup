# Say More conversation editorial standard
Applies to curated *Start Something*, *Keep Talking* and *Play Something* content. This is a production quality gate, not a volume target.

## Intent
The user reads a line privately and naturally brings it into an existing text, call, or in-person conversation. The app is not a chatbot, a relationship interview, or a questionnaire. A good starter can lead to competing opinions, funny stories, surprising memories, disagreement, or follow-ups without requiring anyone to disclose sensitive personal information.

## Publishing criteria
- **Response pull:** Can someone immediately react, disagree, tell a story, laugh or choose a side? A purely biographical information request ("What is your favourite colour?") is not sufficient.
- **Human voice:** Write something a real person could actually send or say. Avoid recurring mechanical stems such as "What is your view on..." and repetitive "Tell me about a time when..." questions. Vary formats: an observation, a plausible scenario, a genuine hot take, a quick choice or a story opening.
- **Continuity:** Every curated conversation has at least three *distinct* follow-up angles. They should open different directions: personal anecdote, counterpoint, comparison, unexpected tangent or changed stakes. Do not produce four synonyms of the opening question.
- **Context fit:** Tag by topic, suitable audiences and conversation vibe. Flirty prompts are not family-safe by default. Do not assume all users share the same religion, relationship status, age or cultural experience. Nigerian references can enrich the mix without being forced into every card.
- **No generic padding:** Never inflate counts by changing a name, swapping synonyms, or templating hundreds of near-identical questions. Reject exact and meaningful semantic duplicates.
- **Truthful provenance:** Editor-authored or AI-reviewed content must not be falsely presented as human-reviewed. Keep the older published collections and saved IDs intact; do not replace the release catalogue to improve a number on the screen.
- **Games:** No round numbers, scores, points, streaks, winner screens or forced restart loops. Every card stands alone; the user may skip, select another card or switch games at any time. Cards must work in their supported Text, Call or In person formats. A remote dare must never depend on touching or filming someone.
- **Privacy and safety:** No forced disclosures of credentials, financial details, embarrassing private histories or intimate imagery. Adult-only prompts remain behind their separate opt-in and must never be used in general games.

## Reviewer checklist
Read every line aloud. Ask: Would I use this myself? Can someone answer with more than a single word? Does it sound distinct from the neighbouring cards? Are the follow-up paths genuinely different? Would this be comfortable with the selected audience and medium? Only retain a card when all checks pass.

## Preview catalogue
The conversation-first preview keeps 12 original editorial pilot starters and adds 37 individually authored starters spanning the 18 retained conversation subtopics, each with four continuations. It keeps ten original casual game cards and adds 96 individually written cards, including Story Chain and format-/audience-specific prompts. The pre-existing curated batch collections and separate Truth or Dare data remain unchanged. These new pilot texts are AI-authored proposals pending independent human editorial review; automated tests cover structural quality and exact overlap, not nuanced editorial judgement.
